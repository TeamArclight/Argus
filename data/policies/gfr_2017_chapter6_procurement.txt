GENERAL FINANCIAL RULES (GFR) 2017
GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
MINISTRY OF FINANCE, DEPARTMENT OF EXPENDITURE

CHAPTER 6: PROCUREMENT OF GOODS AND SERVICES

Rule 149: Fundamental Principles of Public Buying
Every authority delegated with the financial powers of procuring goods in the
public interest shall have the responsibility and accountability to bring
efficiency, economy, and transparency in matters relating to public procurement.

Rule 153: Purchase of Goods — Open Tender Enquiry
Save in cases of proprietary articles or single source supply, open tender
enquiry is the most preferred method of procurement.

Rule 154: Mandatory Use of GeM
All Ministries and Departments shall procure goods and services available on
Government e-Marketplace (GeM). Procurement of goods or services not available
on GeM shall follow the procedure prescribed under GFR Rules 155-170.

Rule 155: Registration on GeM
All bidders must be registered on the GeM portal before submitting bids.
Registration requires a valid GSTIN, PAN, and bank account details.

Rule 156: Financial Qualification
(a) The procuring authority shall prescribe minimum annual turnover criteria
    based on the estimated value of procurement.
(b) For procurements up to INR 10 Crore, minimum annual turnover may be set
    at 30% of the estimated value.
(c) For procurements above INR 10 Crore, minimum annual turnover may be set
    at 50% of the estimated value, subject to the Competent Authority's discretion.

Rule 157: Technical Qualification
(a) Bidders shall demonstrate relevant experience in similar work.
(b) Minimum experience of 3 years in the relevant field shall be prescribed
    for procurements above INR 1 Crore.
(c) Experience certificates from Central Government, State Government, PSU,
    or autonomous body clients shall be accepted.

Rule 158: Eligibility Restrictions
(a) Firms that have been debarred or blacklisted by any Central or State
    Government department are not eligible to participate in government
    procurement for the period of debarment.
(b) A self-declaration of non-blacklisting shall be obtained from all bidders.
(c) The procuring authority shall verify the debarment status through the
    centralized debarment database maintained by the Department of Expenditure.

Rule 159: Verification of Bidder Claims
(a) All factual claims made by bidders in their submissions shall be verified
    through authorized government portals or registries.
(b) Verification shall cover: GSTIN status, Udyam registration, company
    registration with MCA, EPFO/ESIC compliance, and financial claims.
(c) In case of discrepancy between claimed and verified values, the verified
    value shall prevail, and the matter shall be referred to the Competent
    Authority for review.
(d) Verification results from authorized API sources shall be given precedence
    over self-declared or document-based evidence.

Rule 160: Turnover Exemptions for MSMEs
(a) Micro and Small Enterprises registered under the MSME Development Act shall
    be exempt from the prior turnover criteria prescribed under Rule 156.
(b) This exemption applies only to MSMEs registered under the Udyam portal with
    a valid and current Udyam Registration Certificate.
(c) Medium enterprises and large companies are not eligible for this exemption.

Rule 161: EMD Exemption
(a) MSMEs registered on the Udyam portal and startups registered under DPIIT
    are exempt from submitting Earnest Money Deposit (EMD).

Rule 162: Bid Evaluation
(a) Technical evaluation shall be completed before opening financial bids.
(b) All evaluation criteria shall be disclosed in the tender document.
(c) The procuring authority shall maintain a complete audit trail of all
    evaluation decisions, including reasons for qualification and disqualification.
(d) Any deviation from prescribed evaluation criteria requires prior approval
    of the Competent Authority.

Rule 163: Award of Contract
The contract shall be awarded to the lowest evaluated responsive bidder (L1)
who meets all technical and eligibility criteria.
