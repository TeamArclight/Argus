GOVERNMENT e-MARKETPLACE (GeM)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE FOR BID COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION

Version 3.2 | Effective Date: 01/04/2026

1. PURPOSE
This SOP establishes the procedure for verifying bidder eligibility and
compliance claims submitted on the GeM platform.

2. SCOPE
Applicable to all procurement officers evaluating bids on GeM for goods
and services valued above INR 25,00,000.

3. DOCUMENT VERIFICATION HIERARCHY

3.1 Verification Priority Order:
    Priority 1: Authorized government API/registry response
    Priority 2: Portal-cached verified data (less than 90 days old)
    Priority 3: Document-based verification (certificates, statements)
    Priority 4: Self-declaration by bidder

3.2 In case of conflict between verification sources, the higher-priority
    source shall prevail.

4. MANDATORY CHECKS

4.1 GST Verification:
    - Verify GSTIN through the authorized GST portal API
    - Confirm registration status is "Active"
    - Verify legal name matches bidder declaration
    - Confirm state code matches address

4.2 Financial Verification:
    - Verify annual turnover against CA certificate
    - Cross-reference with MCA filings where available
    - For MSMEs, confirm Udyam registration for turnover exemption
    - Turnover figures shall be in Indian Rupees (INR)

4.3 Experience Verification:
    - Verify work completion certificates are issued by authorized officers
    - Confirm contract values and completion dates
    - Minimum experience period is measured from the date of first completed
      contract in the relevant domain

4.4 Debarment Check:
    - Check the centralized debarment database
    - Verify self-declaration against database records
    - Any discrepancy shall result in automatic referral for review

4.5 Labour Compliance:
    - Verify EPFO registration for establishments with 20+ employees
    - Verify ESIC registration for establishments in applicable zones
    - Document-based verification is acceptable for EPFO/ESIC

5. RISK INDICATORS

5.1 The following conditions shall be flagged as risk signals requiring
    officer review:
    (a) SHA-256 hash of submitted document matches a previously submitted
        document from a different bidder
    (b) Entity name on verification does not match bidder registration
    (c) Multiple GSTIN values found across bidder documents
    (d) Certificate expiry date is before bid submission deadline
    (e) Claimed turnover exceeds verified turnover by more than 10%
    (f) Document content is near-identical to another bidder's submission
    (g) OEM authorization letter matches a previously submitted authorization
    (h) Contradictory values extracted from different documents for the
        same data field

5.2 Risk flags do not automatically disqualify a bidder. They require
    manual review by the procurement officer.

6. COMPLIANCE EVALUATION OUTCOMES

6.1 Each requirement evaluation shall result in one of:
    - PASS: Bidder meets the requirement based on verified evidence
    - FAIL: Bidder does not meet the requirement
    - REVIEW_REQUIRED: Conflicting evidence, low confidence, or risk flag
    - UNKNOWN: Insufficient evidence to determine compliance
    - NOT_APPLICABLE: Requirement does not apply to this bidder category

6.2 UNKNOWN shall never be treated as FAIL. It shall be escalated for
    manual review and additional document collection.

6.3 The final qualification/disqualification decision rests with the
    authorized procurement officer. Automated systems provide
    recommendation and evidence only.

7. AUDIT TRAIL

7.1 All verification actions, decisions, and overrides shall be recorded
    in the audit log with:
    - Timestamp
    - Officer ID
    - Action taken
    - Reason code
    - Supporting evidence references

7.2 Audit records shall be retained for a minimum of 5 years.
